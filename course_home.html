A note from Dr. Rennie
The CBC is your gateway into clinical reasoning

If you master only one foundational lab in this course, make it the CBC. It is on every patient’s chart for the rest of the term, and it rewards close reading more than almost any other panel. Take your time with the red cell line trainer. The rest of the course will be easier for it.

Dr. Sharilyn Rennie
