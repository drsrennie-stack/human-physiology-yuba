02
How Units Work

Every unit follows one of two paces. One-week units run a single acquire-apply-demonstrate cycle. Two-week units separate acquire from apply, so you have time to build the knowledge before you use it.

Pace A
One-Week Units

Focused topics with clear boundaries. Concept, trainer, lab, submit, all inside one week.

Monday
Concept launch video, cognitive trainer opens
Tue to Thu
Trainer research and hand work, discussion posts
Friday
Trainer submission due, Tier 2 focused lab opens
Sunday
Lab artifact and short oral defense due
Pace B
Two-Week Units

Complex systems that need depth. Week one builds the knowledge, week two applies it and demonstrates mastery.

Wk 1, Mon
Concept launch, two or three trainers open across the week
Wk 1, Fri
Trainer submissions due, self-check quiz
Wk 2, Mon
Tier 2 focused lab opens, Tier 3 integrated chart later in week
Wk 2, Sun
Full chart, drawing submission, and oral defense due
